Privacy Policy & Fleet Operations

Shree Sai Senthil Travels • Established 1963 • Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Shree Sai Senthil Travels ("we", "our", or "us"). Operating continuously since 1963, we provide tour coach booking, vehicle rental, and fleet management applications (including the Shree Sai Senthil Travels Customer App, Partner App, and Driver Log Portal). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, search for tour coaches, make reservations, or communicate with us:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, and login credentials.
  • Booking & Trip Information: Travel dates, destination city, passenger count, itinerary / places to cover, and assigned coach details.
  • Partner Business Details (Partner App): Company name, business address, operator contact details, and vehicle fleet specifications.
  • Payment Information: Transaction identifiers and payment status (payments are processed securely through certified PCI-DSS payment gateways).

3. Detailed Fleet Information & Telemetry Tracking

Shree Sai Senthil Travels operates a dedicated commercial fleet across Tamil Nadu and South India. Our fleet safety and data standards include:

Fleet Categories & Coach Specifications

  • Ultra Luxury AC Coaches: 40-49 passenger capacity, full climate control, air suspension, and ergonomic push-back seats.
  • Heritage Sleeper Coaches: 30-36 berth configurations for overnight pilgrimage and long-distance heritage routes.
  • Executive Mini-Coaches: 20-26 capacity coaches designed for corporate logistics, family reunions, and regional tours.
  • Classic Non-AC Coaches: 50-54 high-capacity durable transit coaches for large-scale institutional and community tours.

Vehicle Telemetry & GPS Tracking

  • Satellite Location Telemetry: Anonymized GPS location coordinates, speed, and heading are transmitted during active trips to provide live arrival projections and emergency support.
  • Speed Governors & Safety: In compliance with Central Motor Vehicle regulations, all coaches are equipped with certified electronic speed limiters and first-aid safety equipment.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate vehicle searches, tour reservations, and trip management.
  • To generate and provide official booking agreements and travel confirmations.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To send transactional updates, trip confirmations, and customer support notifications.
  • To enable fleet operators to coordinate logistics for booked coaches.
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulatory transport standards.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary information with:

  • Transport Partners & Drivers: To provide trip details required for vehicle assignment and pickup coordination.
  • Payment Processors: To complete secure online and UPI payment transactions.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable transport laws or legal compliance.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures (including SSL encryption) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time. To request data deletion or account removal, please reach out to us at support@shreesaisenthiltravels.com.
